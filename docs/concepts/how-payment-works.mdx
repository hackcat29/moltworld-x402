---
title: "How payment works"
description: "The x402 flow on Moltworld, from quote to receipt."
---

Moltworld uses [x402](https://x402.org), the HTTP 402 payment protocol, with the `exact` scheme on Algorand. Payments are USDC transfers to Moltworld's published address, verified and settled by the GoPlausible facilitator.

## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the request without payment">
    `POST` the JSON body to the model endpoint. Moltworld checks the body first. If it is invalid you get `400` and nothing is asked of you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive 402 Payment Required">
    For a valid body the response is `402` with a `Payment-Required` header. It is base64 JSON listing the network, the USDC asset, the `payTo` address and the exact amount for this body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the payment">
    Your client builds a USDC asset transfer for that amount and signs it. Nothing is sent to the chain yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the request again with Payment-Signature">
    Send the same body with the signed payment in the `Payment-Signature` header. Moltworld has the facilitator verify it, runs the model and settles the payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the result and the receipt">
    The response carries `Payment-Response` (the settlement), `Moltworld-Request-Id` and `Moltworld-Receipt`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The body you pay for must be the body you send. The price is tied to the body, so changing it between the 402 and the paid retry produces a different price and the payment is rejected.
</Note>

## When the payment settles

| Modality | Settles | If the model fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chat | After the answer is produced | The payment is never settled |
| Image, voice | Before the model runs | Refunded on-chain |
| Video | Before the job starts | Refunded on-chain when the job fails |
| Marketplace | After the creator's API answers | The payment is never settled |

## Retries and duplicate payments

A payment is identified by its Algorand transaction id. If your connection drops after you paid, send the same request with the same `Payment-Signature`. You get the original response back with `Moltworld-Replay: true` and you are not charged again. A payment buys one request only: it can never pay for a second one, and a payment that is still being processed is refused with `duplicate_payment_in_flight`.

## Parallel requests

Unsettled requests from one wallet are capped at that wallet's USDC balance on-chain. This stops a burst of parallel requests from spending more than the wallet holds.

## Response headers

| Header | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Payment-Required` | On 402: what to pay, base64 JSON |
| `Payment-Response` | On success: the settlement result |
| `Moltworld-Request-Id` | Id to look up the receipt, job or refund |
| `Moltworld-Receipt` | Signed receipt, base64url JSON. See [Receipts](/concepts/receipts) |
| `Moltworld-Quote` | The price of this body, such as `$0.0618` |
| `Moltworld-Replay` | `true` when the response was replayed for a reused payment |
| `Moltworld-Refund` | `pending` when the model failed after settlement and a refund was queued |
